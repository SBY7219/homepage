施秉佚 SHI Bingyi

I am a student of statistics and economics, interested in how probability, statistical inference, and econometric reasoning help us make sense of empirical questions.

I am completing a B.Sc. in Statistics at Nankai University and will begin a Ph.D. in Economics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2026.

This site collects my research, formal writing, and working notes in mathematics, statistics, economics, logic, and computation.
