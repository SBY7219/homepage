## Statistical inference

Work from model assumptions to estimates and tests.

1. Define the model and sample: [统计模型 (PDF)](/notes/%E7%BB%9F%E8%AE%A1%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B.pdf) and [总体和样本 (PDF)](/notes/%E6%80%BB%E4%BD%93%E5%92%8C%E6%A0%B7%E6%9C%AC.pdf).
2. Study sampling distributions: [抽样分布 (PDF)](/notes/%E6%8A%BD%E6%A0%B7%E5%88%86%E5%B8%83.pdf) and [正态总体下样本均值和样本方差的分布 (PDF)](/notes/%E6%AD%A3%E6%80%81%E6%80%BB%E4%BD%93%E4%B8%8B%E6%A0%B7%E6%9C%AC%E5%9D%87%E5%80%BC%E5%92%8C%E6%A0%B7%E6%9C%AC%E6%96%B9%E5%B7%AE%E7%9A%84%E5%88%86%E5%B8%83.pdf).
3. Compare estimation methods: [极大似然估计 (PDF)](/notes/%E6%9E%81%E5%A4%A7%E4%BC%BC%E7%84%B6%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1.pdf), [贝叶斯估计 (PDF)](/notes/%E8%B4%9D%E5%8F%B6%E6%96%AF%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1.pdf), and [广义矩估计 (PDF)](/notes/%E5%B9%BF%E4%B9%89%E7%9F%A9%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1.pdf).
4. Study a test problem: [双样本不同均值不同方差检验 (PDF)](/notes/%E5%8F%8C%E6%A0%B7%E6%9C%AC%E4%B8%8D%E5%90%8C%E5%9D%87%E5%80%BC%E4%B8%8D%E5%90%8C%E6%96%B9%E5%B7%AE%E6%A3%80%E9%AA%8C.pdf).

The [probability and statistics index](/probstat.html) lists all PDF notes by topic.

## Types and proofs

Start with proofs and typed programs. Then study dependent types.

1. [Curry–Howard 同构讲义](/curry-howard.html): propositions, proofs, and typed terms.
2. [证明与类型](/prot.html): typed calculi and proof systems.
3. [The Little Typer](/little_typer.html): dependent types through code and dialogue.
4. [同伦类型论](/hott.html): identity types and their mathematical interpretation.

## Language implementation

Follow a language from syntax and expansion to evaluation and compilation.

1. [关于程序设计的思考](/programming.html): abstraction and composition.
2. [从宏到 DSL：Racket 的演化](/macro_dsl.html): syntax, macros, and language construction.
3. [Scheme 的三种实现模型](/timp.html): evaluation and implementation models.
4. [P423：编译器构造](/p423.html): passes and representations in a compiler.

For mathematical models of program behavior, read the [指称语义学讲义](/denotational_semantics_notes.html).
