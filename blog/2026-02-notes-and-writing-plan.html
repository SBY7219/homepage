Statistical inference

Work from model assumptions to estimates and tests.

  1. Define the model and sample: 统计模型 (PDF) and 总体和样本 (PDF).
  2. Study sampling distributions: 抽样分布 (PDF) and 正态总体下样本均值和样本方差的分布 (PDF).
  3. Compare estimation methods: 极大似然估计 (PDF), 贝叶斯估计 (PDF), and 广义矩估计 (PDF).
  4. Study a test problem: 双样本不同均值不同方差检验 (PDF).

The probability and statistics index lists all PDF notes by topic.

Types and proofs

Start with proofs and typed programs. Then study dependent types.

  1. Curry–Howard 同构讲义: propositions, proofs, and typed terms.
  2. 证明与类型: typed calculi and proof systems.
  3. The Little Typer: dependent types through code and dialogue.
  4. 同伦类型论: identity types and their mathematical interpretation.

Language implementation

Follow a language from syntax and expansion to evaluation and compilation.

  1. 关于程序设计的思考: abstraction and composition.
  2. 从宏到 DSL：Racket 的演化: syntax, macros, and language construction.
  3. Scheme 的三种实现模型: evaluation and implementation models.
  4. P423：编译器构造: passes and representations in a compiler.

For mathematical models of program behavior, read the 指称语义学讲义.
